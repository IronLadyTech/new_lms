## 1. Company Overview

**Iron Lady** is a women’s leadership development platform founded on **Business War Tactics** — practical strategies for navigating bias, politics, and glass ceilings in corporate and entrepreneurial careers.

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Website** | https://iamironlady.com |
| **Tagline** | Enabling / Elevating A Million Women to the Top |
| **Founder & Director** | Rajesh Bhat |
| **CEO** | Suvarna Hegde |
| **Co-Founders** | Simon Newman (Chairman), Sridhar Sambandam (Director) |
| **Legal name** | Magic Wand Empowerment Private Limited |
| **Scale claims** | 78,000+ women trained · 1,200+ workshops · 100+ women reached ₹1 Cr+ income · 4.8★ Google rating (512+ reviews) |
| **Partners (cited)** | TISS, NSDC, NASSCOM Foundation |
| **Delivery model** | Classroom + live online + app-based learning + mentoring + peer communities |

Iron Lady positions itself against generic “empowerment” language. The brand emphasizes **winning**, **maximizing** (not “balancing”), **shameless pitching**, and **results in minimum time** — framed as corporate warfare tactics adapted for women leaders.

### Leadership Essentials Program (LEP)

**URL:** `/individualPrograms/Leadership_Essentials_Program`

**For whom:** Women told to “balance,” feel guilty about ambition, face politics/bias; early–mid career growth.

**Outcomes:**
- Shameless pitching and strategic maximization
- Crucibles of leadership personal transformation
- BHAG-based fast-track growth plan
- Personal branding (social profile, resume, leadership persona)
- Strengths/weaknesses assessment + skill roadmap
- Iron Lady certification

**Structure:**
- Buddy group (15–20) with mentor
- 27 Business War Tactics practice sessions
- 2 full days live on Zoom + 4 weeks weekly challenges
- Daily 10-min buddy activities
- 1-year community membership

**Modules:** Building Leader Belief → Business War Tactics → Maximizing over Balancing → Extraordinary Brand

## 11. Overall Design Language

### Visual & UX traits
- **Bold empowerment copy** with high-contrast red/black palette
- Cream/beige warmth balanced with strong red CTAs
- Angular clip-path cards on program detail pages
- Hero image carousels with full-bleed photography
- Testimonial carousels + embedded Google Reviews (4.8★)
- Stats-driven social proof blocks (78K+, 100+, 1,200+)
- AOS scroll animations
- React SPA with Material UI components

### Tone of voice
- Direct, provocative, anti-“balance” framing
- Military/strategy metaphors (war tactics, commanding, authority)
- Aspirational income and title outcomes (C-suite, ₹1 Cr+)
- Community-centric (“non-judgmental,” “celebrate ambitions”)

### Imagery themes
- Women leaders on stage, in cohorts, at events
- Red/coral hero banners with bold uppercase headlines
- Event photography gallery (“Glimpses from our Programs”)
