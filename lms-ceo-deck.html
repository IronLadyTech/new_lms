How a learner progresses

  • She sees only the programme she is enrolled in, and resumes where she left off.
  • Each phase unlocks in sequence — no skipping ahead.
  • She must watch 90% of a lesson before the assignment opens.

The differentiator

Every submission is coached, not just marked

This is what separates the LMS from a video library — a human reviews the work and the participant must act on the feedback.

  • Approved → she moves on. Needs improvement or Rejected → she revises and resubmits.
  • Feedback is mandatory when work is sent back — the system will not allow an empty rejection.
  • Work sent back does not count as complete and holds the next lesson closed.

Operations

How CX runs a cohort

  • Each CX member sees only their own programme and their own batches.
  • They review submissions, send reminders, and upload session recordings per batch.
  • Attendance and completion are tracked per participant.
