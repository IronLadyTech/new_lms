# Page Override: CX Home (`/cx/home`)

## Purpose

Single starting point for non-technical CX staff. Answer one question: **“What needs my attention today?”**

## Layout (top → bottom)

1. **Page header** — “Hi, {name}” + one-line guide (no jargon like “command center”)
2. **Summary numbers** — Participants · Needs your attention · Tasks completed  
   - **Read-only** — not clickable (navigation is the left menu only)
3. **Needs your attention** — one combined list:
   - Waiting for resubmit (with Remind)
   - Ready to review (Review button)
   - Plain-language status pills
4. **Your batches** — simple list: name, learner count, Remind; tap name to open batch

## Do NOT on Home

- No “Quick actions” card (duplicates sidebar)
- No “Open review queue” footer links (use **Reviews** in menu)
- No module tracking grid (lives on **Analytics**)
- No KPI cards that link to other pages

## Navigation

Use the icon menu only: Home · Analytics · Batches · Reviews · Profile

## Empty states

Plain English: “You’re all caught up” / “Go to **Batches** in the menu to create one”

## Tone

Friendly, direct, minimal training required.
