Iron Lady LMS

Product Walkthrough · Learner, CX & Admin

Part A

The Learner Experience

What your participants see and do

Learner · Slide 1

Signing in

  1. Open the LMS and sign in with your registered email and password.
  2. You can also continue as a guest to preview the programs.
  3. Guests see all three programs but cannot open any content.
Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/app/home
Learner · Slide 2

Your dashboard

  1. After signing in you land on Home, with a greeting and your program badge.
  2. Quick stats show open programs, pending assignments, upcoming events and recent actions.
  3. "Continue learning" takes you straight back to where you left off.
  4. Scroll down for your programs, announcements and progress.
Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/app/home
Learner · Slide 3

Opening your program

  1. Tap your program on Home to open its journey.
  2. The header shows your cohort, overall progress and the next lesson.
  3. "Your journey" lists every phase — each opens only after the previous one is complete.
  4. Locked phases show a padlock with the reason.
Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/app/home
Learner · Slide 4

Watching a lesson

  1. Open a lesson to see its video, description and any templates to download.
  2. Watch the video — the counter shows how much you have completed.
  3. The submission form stays locked until you reach 90%.
  4. For YouTube videos, tap "I finished watching" to unlock it.

The 90% rule makes sure the lesson is watched before work is submitted.

Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/app/home
Learner · Slide 5

Submitting your work

  1. Once the video is watched, the submission form unlocks.
  2. Download any template provided, complete it, then choose your file.
  3. Tap Submit — a progress bar appears while the file uploads, and you can cancel it.
  4. You'll see "Submitted" with your uploaded file, and Next lesson opens up.
Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/app/home
Learner · Slide 6

Tracking your progress

  1. Progress shows your programs, resources, streak and overall completion.
  2. Tap any stat at the top to jump to that section.
  3. "Continue to study" resumes at your exact next task.
  4. Resources can be filtered by program and opened in a new tab.
Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/app/home
Learner · Slide 7

Calendar, support & profile

  1. Calendar — classes, deadlines and meetings; add any event to Google Calendar.
  2. Support — raise a ticket, reply to staff, and track it until resolved.
  3. Profile — change your display name, switch light/dark mode, and sign out.
Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/app/home
Part B

The CX Workspace

How the Customer Expression team runs a cohort

CX · Slide 1

CX home

  1. Sign in as a CX member to land on your program's workspace.
  2. The top row shows participants, items needing attention, and task completion.
  3. "Needs your attention" lists submissions ready to review or waiting on a resubmit.
  4. Your batches sit on the right, each with a Remind button.
Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/cx/home
CX · Slide 2

The review queue

  1. Open Reviews to see every submission for your program.
  2. Filter by All, Needs review, or Awaiting resubmit.
  3. Narrow further by batch.
  4. Open a row to review the learner's work and choose an outcome.

Approved → learner moves on. Needs improvement or Rejected → feedback is required, and the learner is notified to resubmit.

Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/cx/home
CX · Slide 3

Reviewing a submission

  1. Open a submission to see exactly what the learner sent — text, links, files, video or a template.
  2. Choose an outcome: Approved, Needs improvement, or Rejected.
  3. Feedback is required for Needs improvement and Rejected — the form will not save without it.
  4. Save the review. The learner is notified and can revise and resubmit.
Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/cx/home
CX · Slide 4

Managing batches

  1. Create a batch and give it a name and description.
  2. Add learners, move them between batches, or remove them.
  3. Upload session recordings for each phase — only that batch can see them.
  4. Track attendance and completion per learner.
Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/cx/home
CX · Slide 5

Analytics

  1. Analytics shows cohort health across your whole program.
  2. Charts cover the learner journey, payment status, activity and task completion.
  3. Click any bar or segment to see exactly which participants it represents.
  4. Switch tabs for a per-batch or per-task breakdown.
Part C

Administration

Managing users, content and integrations

Admin · Slide 1

Admin overview

  1. The overview summarises accounts, learners, courses, resources and enrolments.
  2. Open tickets are flagged at the top.
  3. Charts break down users by role, ticket status, activity and signups.
  4. Export any of it to CSV for reporting.
Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/admin
Admin · Slide 2

Users & roles

  1. Search any account by name, email or role.
  2. Change a role: User, Customer Expression, Admin, or Super Admin.
  3. Assign a CX member to their program.
  4. Block or unblock a learner — staff accounts are protected.
Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/admin
Admin · Slide 3

Courses & resources

  1. Create a course with a code, description, thumbnail and intro video.
  2. Add resources by link or by uploading a PDF or PPT.
  3. Lock a resource to hide it from learners until you are ready.
Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/admin
Admin · Slide 4

Batches & enrolment

  1. Create a batch, choose its program, and assign CX leads.
  2. Add learners to the batch.
  3. Add a course to the batch — this is how learners get enrolled.
  4. Move members between batches or remove them.
Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/admin
Admin · Slide 5

Announcements & events

  1. Publish an announcement to everyone, or tag specific learners.
  2. Choose how long it stays visible: 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days.
  3. Add calendar events with a type, joining link and image.
  4. Both appear immediately on the learner's Home and Calendar.
Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/admin
Admin · Slide 6

Zoho CRM integration

  1. Programme, payment status and login credentials flow from Zoho into the LMS.
  2. Test connection checks the CRM link is healthy.
  3. Provision creates or updates an LMS account from a Zoho record.
  4. Browse Zoho Leads and IL Users to find anyone in the CRM.
Open it here:https://new-lms-three-topaz.vercel.app/admin
